InternIQ 🚀

From Skills to Opportunities, From Internships to Success.

InternIQ is an intelligent internship management platform designed to connect students, companies, faculty mentors, Training & Placement (T&P) teams, and administrators on one platform.

It simplifies the complete internship journey—from student registration and eligibility checking to application, selection, progress tracking, completion, and PPO—while adding intelligent features such as skill-gap analysis, internship recommendations, readiness scoring, and actionable analytics.

🎯 Problem

Internship management is often spread across forms, spreadsheets, emails, messages, and documents. This creates:

Repeated data entry
Manual eligibility verification
Difficulty tracking applications
Scattered internship documents
Limited visibility for faculty and T&P teams
Difficulty analyzing internship outcomes

InternIQ brings these processes together into a single connected ecosystem.

🤖 Innovation

InternIQ goes beyond basic internship management through an Intelligence Layer.

1. Internship Readiness Score

Analyzes a student's profile, skills, projects, certifications and eligibility to provide an overall internship-readiness indication.

2. Skill-Gap Analysis

Compares student skills with internship requirements and identifies missing skills.

3. Smart Internship Matching

Recommends suitable internships based on student qualifications and internship requirements.

4. Next Best Action

Provides actionable recommendations to users.

Student: Improve Docker skills to increase internship matches.

Faculty: Follow up with students who have missed reports.

T&P: Identify major skill gaps across departments.

5. QR-Based Verification

Optional QR-based verification can help authenticate offer letters or internship documents.
